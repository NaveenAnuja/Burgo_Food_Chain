01Who we are

Bakery, lounge and restaurant — under one roof.

Burgo Food Chain PVT LTD is home to a bakery, the Iceburg Shisha Lounge and Saivar Restaurant, all at 380/1 Galle Road, Hettimulla.

Each space has its own character, so there is a Burgo for every part of the day — a quick stop at the bakery counter, a sit-down meal with family, or a slow evening in the lounge with friends.

When you would rather eat at home, our live menu is a tap away, ready for you to browse and order online.
