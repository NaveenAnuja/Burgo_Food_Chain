Our Story

The journey of Burgo Food Chain

Welcome to Burgo Food Chain

Burgo Food Chain is more than just a restaurant—it's a culinary destination where diverse dining experiences come together under one roof. Located in the heart of Beruwala on Galle Road, we've built a reputation for delivering exceptional quality, authentic flavors, and memorable experiences.

What makes Burgo special is our unique approach to dining. Rather than offering a single concept, we've created multiple distinct experiences that cater to different moods, occasions, and cravings. From our artisan bakery to our authentic Saivar Restaurant, from casual coffee to the relaxed ambiance of Iceburg Shisha Lounge—each space tells its own story while sharing our commitment to excellence.

"At Burgo, we believe that food is more than sustenance—it's an experience that brings people together, creates memories, and celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Sri Lanka."

Our Philosophy

Quality is at the heart of everything we do. We carefully source our ingredients, support local farmers and suppliers, and maintain the highest standards in food preparation and service. Our chefs combine traditional techniques with modern innovation to create dishes that honor authenticity while embracing creativity.

We're proud to serve both locals and visitors, offering a taste of Sri Lankan hospitality alongside diverse culinary traditions. Whether you're here for a quick coffee and pastry, a full Sri Lankan meal, or an evening of relaxation, you'll find that Burgo is designed with your comfort and satisfaction in mind.
