How to Find Us

🚗 By Car

We're located on Galle Road (A2), the main coastal highway. Easy access from Colombo, Galle, and surrounding areas.

  • • From Colombo: 60 km south on Galle Road
  • • From Galle: 80 km north on Galle Road
  • • Ample parking available

🚌 By Public Transport

Easily accessible by bus along the Galle Road route.

  • • Multiple bus routes stop nearby
